# Surface brief: the app (one screen)

**Scope and mode:** the whole product app, one screen with layers. Operate.

**Audience and job:** people paid in USDC/USDT, on a phone inside their wallet's browser. A returning user reads position, invested, to invest, next buy and this week's cap in seconds and leaves; a new user connects, declares eligibility and moves on to onboarding.

**Chosen direction:** "The terracotta band". Approved comp `.impeccable/mocks/decision/terracotta-band.png`, seed 77e90a30, 2026-09-25 (the surface round put three visualized structures on the table; locking this one approves its comp). Build path: comp-led.

**Structure:** top bar (L symbol · DEVNET chip · EN / ES · wallet chip) inside a terracotta band that owns the upper ~45% of the phone screen and carries the primary figure of the current state in giant cream Archivo; the band ends in a course of kiln-red bricks, with the Seal riding the seam; below on lime, quiet label/value rows separated by sand rules, then brick-shaped buttons at the foot. History, settings, pause, exit and the Telegram link open as layers above this screen.

**States the shell owns (04-01):** not connected (the band carries the one action: connect a wallet; Phantom, Solflare, Backpack), connecting, wrong network or wallet error, eligibility declaration (a layer), blocked country (the band states it plainly; nothing to proceed), connected-not-enrolled (hands off to onboarding), connected-enrolled frame (the numbers themselves are 04-04). Desktop: the phone composition centered and composed, not stretched.

**Memorable moment:** the Seal on the seam between band and rows, stamped with the permission state.

**Risk to manage:** a terracotta block that large must read as the brand, never as an alert: danger and errors use cold crimson with an icon, never the band.

**Unresolved:** final app URL; commissioned rasters (none required for the shell).
